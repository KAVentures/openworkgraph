Organization inventory

Organization design readiness

Inspect saved designs for missing declarations and outstanding review. Findings can be intentional; they do not establish deployment health, tool availability, policy compliance or permission to execute. Counts describe the returned page only.

Organization improvement backlog

Compare scenarios attached to active, independently reviewed designs. Values use the saved volume, time and cost assumptions; they are not measured savings. Scenarios may overlap, so no company savings total is calculated.

Publish or update a reviewed design

Export a reviewed design from OWG's local process catalog and select its JSON file. Previewing the file sends nothing. Publishing shares the model, including baseline labels, goals and declared rules. Raw event histories and local evidence references are excluded.

Shadow evaluation review

Inspect a minimized report from this shared revision's SDK project. Only structural case IDs, hashes, counts and reason codes may be sent to this organization's Gateway. Inspection keeps the report temporary; authenticity, business truth and source outcomes remain unverified.

Check declared rule conditions

Send an authorized case-facts JSON file to this organization's Gateway for an ephemeral check against the selected saved revision. Fact values are not retained or sent to a model provider. Results describe supplied facts, not verified policy compliance or permission to execute.

For decision tables include an explicit as_of_date (YYYY-MM-DD) alongside cases in the JSON. Overlaps, gaps, unknown facts and inactive definitions withhold a target. Facts and reference documents remain unverified.

Revision review

Authors cannot approve their own revision. Saving a new revision requires a new review. Review comments remain user-provided context.
