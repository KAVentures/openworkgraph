Manage redacted browser excerpts shared with your personal OWG account.

Sign in to see your linked computers and sharing status.

Approve one computer

This allows the selected linked computer to upload new redacted page text and drafts only after you also enable capture, local AI access and cloud sharing in its Privacy settings. Older local text is not uploaded.

Excerpts can contain third-party or confidential information despite filtering. Connected cloud AI apps can read them while your computer is offline. They expire within seven days of observation. This does not enable observation on your computer.

Check the device ID against your desktop connection settings. Do not approve an unrecognized computer.

Delete cloud work text

Deletes retained excerpts and approvals for all your computers. Uploads remain blocked until you approve a device again here and grant fresh permission on that computer. Content already shown in an AI conversation cannot be recalled.
