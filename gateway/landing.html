OpenWorkGraph Gateway

Your organization's self-hosted Gateway. It receives only what enrolled computers are allowed to share.

Organization admin console →For IT and Gateway administrators: employees, invitations, sharing policy, retention, access and audit. What your organization holds about you →For employees. Easiest from OpenWorkGraph on your own computer: Organization → See what your organization holds about you.

Your own recorded work stays in OpenWorkGraph on your computer. To join your organization, use the personal invitation your IT admin sent you, in OpenWorkGraph → Organization → Join your organization.
