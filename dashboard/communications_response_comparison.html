Choose two OWG response-episode aggregate JSON exports from the Communications dashboard. Compare observed deadline performance, repeat contacts, workload exposure, coverage, and shared supported intent groups.

Files are processed in this browser only. No raw messages, thread identifiers, network upload, or saved workspace are needed. Input digests identify the selected bytes, not their authenticity.

Comparison basis

Deadline bounds vary only the potential on-time outcomes of pending episodes. They are not confidence intervals and do not account for missing history. Workload exposure is not staffing time. No production SLA or causal improvement is certified.
