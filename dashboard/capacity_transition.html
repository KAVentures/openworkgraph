Demand-period capacity planning

Reuse one existing OWG capacity-grid report to explore a sequence of hypothetical demand periods. Find the minimum declared cost among tested designs that pass the reviewed deadline target in every seed, subject to a switch limit and optional budget.

Independent demand runs do not simulate transitions, carryover queues, shifts, training, or availability. This is a finite scenario calculation, not an actual staffing schedule, observed savings, or certified service guarantee. All files and calculations stay in this browser.
