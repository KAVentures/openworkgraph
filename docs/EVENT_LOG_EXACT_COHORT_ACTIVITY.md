# Exact activity in local cohort forms

The local cohort forms used by KPIs, cohort comparisons, cohort metrics, case tagging, derived logs and saved-graph comparisons suggest up to 500 common activities. Enter an exact activity or use **Find labels** to search the current case notion for a less common one. The exact value overrides the suggestion and preserves literal spaces and Unicode. Choosing a different suggestion clears the exact value. Unknown labels are evaluated as entered and may select no cases; labels longer than 200 Unicode characters are refused.

The case-evidence inspector retains its existing exact activity control. Editing a KPI input or cohort clears its previously displayed result and disables its downloads until recalculation; in-flight responses from older selections cannot restore stale results. Other cohort consumers retain their existing preview and review rules.
