# Exact activities in saved signals

For case signals that watch whether cases contain or lack an activity, the selector suggests up to 500 common labels. Enter an exact label or use **Find labels** to search the current case notion, including less frequent activities. Exact entries preserve spaces and Unicode and override the suggestion; choosing a different suggestion clears the exact entry.

The same literal value is used in the draft preview and saved rule. Editing the selection clears the preview. A label longer than 200 Unicode characters is refused before a preview or save; an unknown valid label is evaluated against the selected log rather than substituted with another activity. Signals remain descriptive and do not authorize business actions.
