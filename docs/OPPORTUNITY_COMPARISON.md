# Comparing opportunities

Select **Compare** on two to four ideas in the local automation pipeline to
inspect their saved assessment bases side by side. The comparison includes
saved revision, state, source declaration, modeled monthly hours, ease,
priority, entered volume/time/eligible share, individual questionnaire answers
and the compliance-review declaration. Missing assessments remain distinct
from assessed zero values.

Search and priority filters may hide selected table rows without removing
those ideas from the explicit comparison. A state refresh removes selected
ideas no longer available in that state. Values are from the last list refresh;
another user’s later changes appear after **Refresh opportunities**. Refresh
preserves the view filters and selection when the selected ideas remain in
the loaded state. **Clear comparison** removes the selection.
Selections are view-only and are not persisted across reloads.

Each idea uses its own declared assumptions. Processes and eligible cases may
overlap, so the comparison supplies no summed benefits or automatic winner.
Compliance declarations do not prove completed reviews. Selection does not
save an assessment, approve an idea, or grant execution permissions.
