# Business support domain packs (R23)

Three additional versioned packs are available through the normal saved-log
domain pack chooser, using exact source activity and case mappings.

| Pack | Intended population | Included review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receivables collections | Selected paid-closure cases | Assignment, outreach, payment and closure timing, promises, dispute and reopen records |
| Employee access requests | Selected ordinary approved requests | Assessment, approval-to-provisioning, verification, repeat provisioning and recorded resource separation |
| Recruitment offer operations | Selected accepted-offer administrative cases | Screening, interview, offer approval, acceptance timing, rescheduling and repeated records |

Each version 1.0.0 includes illustrative elapsed-hour targets, metrics,
precedence/response/cardinality checks, and an illustrative reference
lifecycle. Access requests also include two recorded resource-separation
checks. Optional roles that are not mapped are explicitly not applicable.
The existing installation, metric ownership, revision history, report,
benchmark and uninstall mechanisms are reused unchanged.

Select a population matching the intended path before installing.
Alternative legitimate paths may deviate. Targets are editable examples,
not legal, contractual or organizational requirements. Do not use these
packs to make access, debt collection or hiring decisions. No business
outcomes, account privileges, payment settlement or applicant suitability
are established. Use pseudonymous case identifiers and administrative
events rather than sensitive source contents.

Synthetic CSV regression cases exercise real import, installation,
reporting, optional mapping, target changes, metric ownership and
recorded resource separation. Real-source and CI acceptance remain needed.
