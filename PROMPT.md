# OpenWorkGraph starter prompt

Use the attached OpenWorkGraph evidence to understand how I actually performed my work. Treat raw observations as evidence and derived tasks/summaries as non-authoritative heuristics. Reconstruct the workflow yourself where needed, and clearly distinguish observed facts from your inferences.

Combine this workflow evidence with all other context and tools available to you, such as documents, code repositories, email, project systems, prior conversations, APIs, and enterprise knowledge. Identify what I was doing, recurring processes, friction, unnecessary switching or checking, and opportunities to improve or automate my workflows.

Do not assume that foreground duration equals active work. Use engaged time, interactions, timing, transitions, and the surrounding sequence. If browser-extension events are absent, remember that desktop-derived browser evidence may still be present.

When you recommend an improvement or automation, cite the workflow evidence that supports it and state any uncertainty.
